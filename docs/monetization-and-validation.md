# WoHo Browser Business Validation Plan

**Status:** Hypotheses only; no revenue or demand is assumed.

## Potential customer groups
1. Individual developers who want page-aware AI research.
2. Small teams that need a managed, configurable browser.
3. Organizations that need private/local document assistance.
4. IT service providers who deploy and maintain desktop software.

## Possible offers to validate
- Free browser with optional AI usage limits.
- Paid business deployment, policy configuration, and support.
- Custom AI integration for an organization.
- Paid support or maintenance agreement.
Do not build billing or unlimited AI usage before validating willingness to pay and estimating provider costs.

## Validation steps
1. Interview 10–15 target users about their current workflow and unmet need.
2. Ask what they currently use, what fails, and what they already pay for.
3. Show a small working prototype rather than only a feature list.
4. Request a concrete next step: pilot, written feedback, or paid trial.
5. Track conversion, repeat use, support burden, AI cost per active user, and gross margin.
6. Continue only if a clear problem and reachable buyer emerge.

## Early success metrics
- Successful first launch and first navigation.
- Weekly active testers and repeat usage.
- AI feature use and task completion.
- Crash rate and update success rate.
- Number of qualified pilot conversations and pilots started.
- Willingness-to-pay evidence and support cost.

## Guardrails
- Do not sell browsing history or silently monetize page contents.
- Do not claim guaranteed income, superiority, privacy, or security without evidence.
- Keep a free, useful browsing baseline possible if AI services are unavailable.
